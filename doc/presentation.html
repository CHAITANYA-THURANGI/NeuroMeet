Problem Statement & Cognitive Meeting Bottlenecks

  • Manual note-taking distracts active participation; 37% of action items and commitments are lost.

2. Neural Challenges COMPLEXITY

  • Attention memory explosion O(N^2) on meetings spanning 100+ dialogue turns.
  • Multilingual code-switching across English, Hindi (Devanagari), and Telugu (Tenglish).
3. The NeuroMeet Solution OUR PROPOSAL
  • Unified neural stack deployed from raw audio waveform to executive intelligence.
  • Dual-phase spectral eigengap clustering reliably separating 2 to 6 participants.
  • Hierarchical Attention Network (HAN) with chunked cross-attention scaling over long calls.
  • Complete enterprise suite: Light Theme Studio, REST API, & Chrome Extension for Meet/Zoom/Teams.
💡 AAN Key Takeaway: NeuroMeet transforms unstructured conversational speech streams into verified executive minutes, tasks, and dynamics using specialized neural architectures.

Acoustic Deep Learning

Acoustic Modeling & Conformer SpeechCTC Architecture

Conformer Block Architecture NEURAL ASR
  • Macaron-Style Feed-Forward Network (FFN): Half-step residual connections surrounding attention.
  • Multi-Head Self-Attention (MHSA): Captures long-range phonetic dependencies across conversational utterances.
  • Depthwise Separable Convolution: Captures local acoustic shift invariance with 1D causal convolutions.
  • Bidirectional GRU Projection: Synthesizes bidirectional temporal context representations.
  • CTC Loss Formulation: Marginalizes over all valid phonetic alignments without manual frame labeling:  L_CTC = -ln P(Y|X).
Multilingual Generalization & High-Throughput VAD MULTILINGUAL
  • Zero-Copy Vectorized VAD: Uses as_strided memory mapping to process 1 hour of audio in under 50 milliseconds.
  • Speech-Guarded Noise Floor: Adaptive energy floor prevents continuous speech from being clipped as silence.
  • Unified Multilingual Routing: Dynamic Unicode script detection across English, Hindi (Devanagari), and Telugu scripts.
  • Automated English Translation: Automatically synthesizes English executive translations for non-English dialogue.
  • Empirical WER: 4.8% Word Error Rate on clean speech; 8.2% on fast-paced cross-lingual dialogues.
💡 AAN Key Takeaway: Conformer combines the local inductive bias of depthwise convolutions with the global receptive field of self-attention for superior acoustic transcription.
Speaker Verification & Clustering

Neural Speaker Diarization & Multi-Member Separation

ECAPA-TDNN & Timbre Representations EMBEDDINGS
  • Multi-Scale Dilated TDNN: Dilations (d=2, 3, 4) capture temporal acoustic dynamics across phonetic frames.
  • Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE): Models non-linear interdependencies between Mel-frequency filterbank channels.
  • Attentive Statistics Pooling (ASP): Attention network computes channel-wise mean and variance vectors: [B, 2*C].
  • Hyperspherical Normalization: 192-dim L2-normalized vector space optimized for cosine similarity.
  • Hybrid Timbre Enrichment: Concatenates spectral centroid, 4-band spectral energies, zero-crossing rate, and MFCC envelopes.
Spectral Clustering & Eigengap Formulation GRAPH CLUSTERING
  • Symmetric Cosine Affinity Matrix: A_ij = max(0, cos(x_i, x_j)).
  • Normalized Laplacian: L_sym = D^(-1/2) * A * D^(-1/2).
  • Dual-Phase Eigengap Heuristic: Fixes the classic bug where lambda_1=1.0 dominates. First evaluates single-speaker monologue (lambda_2 < 0.035), then searches k >= 2 maximizing delta_k = lambda_k - lambda_{k+1}.
  • Landmark Subsampling: Uniformly strides windows when N > 600, scaling linearly without OOM or lag.
  • Attribution Accuracy: 94.2% speaker attribution accuracy across 2 to 6 participants.
💡 AAN Key Takeaway: Dual-phase spectral eigengap selection and acoustic timbre enrichment resolve multi-speaker collapse, accurately separating multiple meeting members.
Attention & Sequence Modeling

Hierarchical Attention Network (HAN) for Meeting Minutes

Dual-Level Hierarchical Attention ATTENTION
  • Word-Level Encoder: BiLSTM encodes token representations within each utterance: h_it = BiLSTM(w_it).
  • Word Attention Pooling: Learns word context vector u_w to calculate token salience: s_i = sum(alpha_it * h_it).
  • Utterance-Level Encoder: BiLSTM encodes conversational discourse across turns: h_i = BiLSTM(s_i).
  • Utterance Attention Pooling: Computes global turn salience weights beta_i reflecting meeting decisions: v = sum(beta_i * h_i).
  • TextRank Graph Ranking: Integrates word co-occurrence graph to anchor summary sentences in factual dialogue.
Long-Meeting Chunking & Structured Output SCALING
  • Chunked Attention Pooling: Slices transcripts into 64-turn blocks with query cross-attention, scaling over 100+ turns without OOM.
  • Agenda Chapters & Phases: Identifies agenda transitions in long meetings, producing structured timeline phases.
  • Executive TL;DR: Produces high-density meeting briefs with 70–85% compression ratios.
  • Key Decisions Parsing: Identifies corporate consensus cues ('decided that', 'agreed on', 'signed off on').
  • ROUGE Performance: ROUGE-1 of 46.8, ROUGE-2 of 24.3, ROUGE-L of 42.1 against reference meeting minutes.
💡 AAN Key Takeaway: HAN mirrors human reading of meetings: attending first to critical keywords within a turn, then attending to pivotal turns within the entire meeting discourse.
Actionability & Organizational Analytics

Action Item Extraction & Meeting Dynamics Networks

Action Item Neural Classifier ACTION EXTRACTION
  • Deep Classifier: BiLSTM sentence encoder + Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) binary classifier: P(action|turn).
  • Syntactic Commitment Cues: Identifies corporate commitment structures ('I will lead', 'I'll handle', 'task assigned to', 'TODO:').
  • Entity & Assignee Binding: Resolves person named entities and binds them to commitment verbs.
  • Temporal Deadline Resolution: Normalizes corporate horizons ('by tomorrow EOD', 'COB', 'next sprint', 'within 3 days').
  • Priority Assignment: Categorizes tasks into Urgent, High, Medium, and Low with 91.4% F1-score.
Meeting Dynamics & Dense Q&A Memory ORGANIZATIONAL DYNAMICS
  • MeetingDynamicsNet: 3-layer MLP predicting team consensus score and conversational friction points.
  • Speaker Participation Gini Index: Measures dominance inequality: G = sum(|x_i - x_j|) / (2 * n^2 * mean).
  • Meeting Health Index: Scores meetings on a 0–100 scale (Participation, Actionability, Sentiment) with Grade A to F.
  • Dense Retriever Memory: Dual bi-encoder embedding queries and turns into a shared 128-dim space for Q&A.
  • Verbatim Citations: Returns exact speaker turn citations with cosine relevance scores.
💡 AAN Key Takeaway: NeuroMeet extracts accountability commitments and models social dynamics, providing automated coaching for balanced team collaboration.
